# Solar Steph Space Weather Dashboard

Live at https://forecast.stephyardley.com

A personal project by Dr Steph Yardley showing live space weather: the Sun, the corona, flares, radiation,
solar wind, geomagnetic activity, the aurora oval, and a map of where on the Sun the solar wind at Earth most
likely came from. It is for interest and education and is not an official forecast.
